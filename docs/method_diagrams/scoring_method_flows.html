Method reference

Cell-Line Scoring Methods

How raw expression and protein-abundance measurements are turned into a single, comparable z-score per gene and cell line. Each diagram traces one scorer end to end; the source list below it maps every step back to the function that implements it.

RNA

Transcript Expression Score

Combines three RNA sources — DepMap, HPA, and GEO microarray data spanning many platforms and processing pipelines — into one lineage-conditioned z-score per gene, per cell line.

Raw expression, per source

DepMap RNA-seq (log2 TPM+1) and HPA RNA-seq (TPM) each form one method. GEO microarray samples are split into one method per platform and processing pipeline (RMA, MAS5, gcRMA, …) — different processing pipelines are not on a comparable scale.

Collapse replicate samples

Multiple samples for the same cell line are reduced to one value by taking the median, so a heavily replicated cell line cannot dominate its lineage's statistics.

Scale & detection floor, per method

Each method's scale (log or linear) is confirmed from its own value distribution, not assumed from metadata. RNA-seq sources use a fixed detection floor at the standard ≥1 TPM convention; microarray methods get an empirical floor from their own values. All values are placed on a common log2 scale.

Detection fraction, per gene within each lineage

For each gene, the share of a lineage's cell lines with a value at or above the detection floor decides how that gene is scored in that lineage.

Well detected

Robust z-score (median / MAD) within the lineage. The lineage's own median centres the score where enough cell lines were scored, otherwise the method-wide median is used. The scale is shrunk toward the method-wide scale — more so for smaller lineages.

Partially detected

Rank-based inverse-normal transform of the within-lineage ranks. Cell lines tied at the detection floor share a common rank, so a censored value reads as a lower bound rather than a precise estimate.

Below detection

The gene is left unscored for that lineage — excluded from the analysis, never recorded as a zero.

Lineages with too few scored cell lines are excluded outright for that gene, regardless of detection fraction.

Method-level z-score

One z-score per gene, per cell line, from whichever regime applied to that gene's lineage.

Combine GEO's methods

GEO's platform × processing methods are combined by a weighted average, each method weighted by its sample size.

Combine the three sources

DepMap, HPA, and combined GEO are combined at equal weight — one source, one vote — regardless of how many individual methods feed into GEO.

Protein

Protein Abundance Score

Combines two independent proteomics platforms — ProCAN and CCLE — into one z-score per gene, per cell line, weighting each platform by coverage and by how well the two platforms agree.

Two independent abundance platforms

ProCAN and CCLE proteomics each measure protein abundance per cell line. Neither platform is treated as the reference; both are carried through on equal footing.

Derive agreement thresholds from the data

The correlation needed to call two protein measurements concordant, and the correlation needed to call two platforms in agreement, are not fixed in advance. Each is set by comparing real, matched correlations against a null built from deliberately mismatched proteins and genes, using standard significance and power conventions.

ProCAN — isoform handling

Where a gene has more than one measured protein form, forms that correlate with each other above the derived threshold are combined by the maximum abundance across forms; forms that disagree, or can't be compared, are combined by the mean.

CCLE — isoform handling

Where a gene has more than one measured protein form, forms that correlate with each other above the derived threshold are combined by the maximum abundance across forms; forms that disagree, or can't be compared, are combined by the mean.

Platform-agreement assessment, per gene

ProCAN and CCLE abundance are correlated for each gene, across the cell lines both platforms measured, and compared against the thresholds derived above. This sets a confidence score for the gene, from fully trusted to fully down-weighted. A gene without enough shared cell lines to test defaults to full confidence.

Per-gene z-score, within each platform

A robust (median / MAD) z-score is computed per gene, within each lineage, separately for ProCAN and for CCLE.

Combine platforms

ProCAN and CCLE z-scores are combined by a weighted average. Each platform's weight for a gene reflects how many cell lines that gene was measured in on that platform — broader coverage carries more weight.

Both platforms measured this cell line

The combined score is scaled by the gene's platform-agreement confidence.

Only one platform measured this cell line

The score passes through unweighted — there is no second platform to disagree with.
